This is the first official evaluation phase of the competition. Submissions will be evaluated on the val split of the social-hm3d dataset, which consists of approximately 1,000 RGB-D episodes. The goal is to assess the generalization performance of your model in previously unseen scenes with realistic human motion and layout diversity.

Participants are encouraged to explore advanced model designs, including large pretrained models such as CLIP, VLMs, or vision-language agents. We welcome train-free, zero-shot, or hybrid approaches that leverage general-purpose perception and reasoning capabilities. Use of publicly available pretrained models is allowed, provided they are clearly cited and reproducible.

To ensure fairness, all models must be trained primarily on the provided social-hm3d/train split. The use of private datasets or scene-level priors from the validation split is strictly prohibited. Use of additional public datasets (e.g., iGibson, MP3D trajectories) is permitted only if: (1) the data is fully public and widely accessible, and (2) all details of its usage are clearly cited and documented in the method description for full reproducibility.

Submissions in this phase are run through the full remote evaluation pipeline. Scores for all metrics (SR, SPL, PSC, and H-Coll) will be computed and reported. The resulting performance will be reflected on the public leaderboard.

A maximum of 1 submission per day is allowed in this phase, with a total limit of 10 submissions. Please ensure each submission is meaningful and reproducible.


Note: The leaderboard will display only your best submission.
